About

I am a public health researcher and epidemiologist with over three years of experience in applied research across tobacco control, non-communicable disease epidemiology and health promotion. My work combines field-based data collection, data analysis and scientific writing, and I teach biostatistics and research methodology to undergraduate and graduate students. I founded the LUMEN Research Group to support collaborative, evidence-based public health research.
